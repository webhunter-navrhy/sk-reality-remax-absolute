RE/MAX exclusively offers for sale a luxurious large-scale 3-bedroom apartment in Bratislava's Old Town in the well-known River Park residential project on the banks of the Danube that was never inhabited.

The apartment on the tenth floor out of twelve has a usable area of 184.13 m² plus two terraces with a total area of another 33.82 m². It is finished in a high standard using high-quality materials. Comfort of living is guaranteed by three-chamber aluminum windows combined with electrically controlled exterior blinds and central air conditioning in the entire apartment.

Apartment is oriented to the southwest and offers breathtaking views from all rooms directly on the Danube River. Apartment has one parking space in the underground garage and a storage room and a cellar located directly on the same floor as the apartment.

Layout of the apartment consists of an entrance hall with a toilet for the day part, a separate walk-in wardrobe and a technical room at the entrance. The living room is an open plan consisting of kitchen with bar seating connected to the dining and living room with access to one of the two terraces with a view of the Danube. The night part consists of 3 rooms, each with a built-in wardrobe and its own entrance to the terrace with a view of the Danube. The apartment has two bathrooms with a bathtub, one of them, in addition to a bathtub, also has a shower and a total of 3 toilets. There is a walk-in wardrobe next to the master bedroom and another built-in wardrobe is located in the hallway opposite from the rooms.

The apartment building was approved in 2017. Within walking distance, in its immediate vicinity, there are great amenities like restaurants, cafes, children's playground, promenade, and all this just a few minutes walk from the historic city center. There is also excellent transport accessibility to the city center or the highway bypass and the immediate vicinity of public transport stops.

If you are interested in additional information or a viewing, please do not hesitate to contact us anytime.
